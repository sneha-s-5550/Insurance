AWARD & RECONGNITIONS


Liberty General Insurance since its launch in India has been instrumental at achieving highest standards of customer service, technology implementation and industry contribution. The awards and felicitations received by the organisation bears testimony of the above. Liberty General Insurance has always kept customers at forefront of its focus. Its employees take immense pride in contributing to the success of the company and their individual contributions create the desired differentiation for the brand. Liberty General Insurance has been dedicated towards improving the lives of individuals and entities with its uniquely designed insurance solutions and hence attempts to create safer and secure environment for all. These effort paves way for many successful innovations and targets efficiency in all possible means. We are here to create a responsible and secure environment and hence also are dedicated to become India’s most admired general insurance company through our achievements.



WE'RE PASSINATE.WE'RE BOLD


A joint venture between Liberty Citystate holdings PTE Ltd, a group company of US Headquartered Liberty Mutual Insurance Group, a leading multinational property and casualty group, and Liberty Industries Limited, Liberty General Insurance commenced operations in 2013 with the aim of providing comprehensive retail, commercial and industrial insurance solutions.
